ARTIST STATEMENT

"My work balances power with stillness. A tiger's intense gaze amidst natural flora, or the kinetic spray of ocean water caught on raw paper — capturing natural instinct through sharp, purposeful strokes."

I work between natural physical media — dragging palette knives with heavy oil glazes across linen — and sharp digital illustrations. In every piece, I aim to preserve the raw, tactile presence of nature: organic botanical structures, raw bone values, and deep obsidian shadows.

I. GESTURE & STUDY
OBSERVATION

Studying natural motion, anatomical tension, and organic flora in the field.

II. LAYER & PIGMENT
STRUCTURE

Building rich tonal depth with charcoal grounds, raw umbers, and controlled glazes.

III. REFINEMENT
FINAL POLISH

Refining micro-edges, specular highlights, and archival output.
